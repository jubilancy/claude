## Das Kleine Schmetterlingsbuch. (The Little Butterfly Book.) 

Hübner, Jacob, 1761-1826; Schnack, Friedrich, 1888-1977

Book Details

- **Title:** *Das kleine Schmetterlingsbuch - In vielen Farben*
- **Publisher:** Insel Verlag (originally published in Leipzig around 1934)
- **Illustrations:** Features colored butterfly and day-moth engravings (*Tagfalter*) by the artist Jakob Hübner.
- **Introduction:** Includes a foreword written by Friedrich Schnack.
- **Cover Design:** A classic, collectible Insel-Bücherei paper binding with a repeating colorful butterfly pattern and a paper title label.
