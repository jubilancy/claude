> ### GAME OF THRONES: SEASON 5, EPISODE 7
>
> > Winter is coming. **All men must die**. And [Game of Thrones is back](http://thegetty.tumblr.com/tagged/game-of-thrones/)! Stay tuned each week as we unpack Sunday’s episodes through masterpieces.
>
> A baby’s laugh lulls an old man to a [peaceful death](http://www.getty.edu/art/collection/objects/3925/master-of-james-iv-of-scotland-deathbed-scene-flemish-about-1510-1520/?dz=0.6473,0.4702,1.62), the [snows of winter fall](https://www.google.com/culturalinstitute/asset-viewer/hunters-in-the-snow-winter/WgFmzFNNN74nUg) all around the northern realms, and a faithful ally is [flayed alive](http://www.metmuseum.org/collection/the-collection-online/search/476566). A king considers two mystical royal bloodline options with [the game of thrones board game](http://www.getty.edu/art/collection/objects/264567/lieven-van-lathem-david-aubert-initial-c-gillion's-sons-kneeling-before-the-king-of-cyprus-flemish-after-1464/?dz=0.1788,0.1951,2.82) before him. 
>
> A [wolf](http://www.getty.edu/art/collection/objects/4649/unknown-maker-a-man-leading-a-horse-to-two-other-men-a-wolf-before-a-flock-of-sheep-and-rams-german-third-quarter-of-15th-century/?dz=0.5288,1.1208,1.57) aids a weary fighter, slaves compete like [gladiators](http://www.getty.edu/art/collection/objects/29497/unknown-maker-statuette-of-a-gladiator-roman-about-50/), and an irate son makes [demands of his mother](http://www.getty.edu/art/collection/objects/4973/unknown-maker-gaheriet-speaking-to-queen-iseult-about-tristan-french-about-1320-1340/?dz=0.7098,0.2754,1.72). A seductively venomous prison scene ends with a fortuitous antidote to a [powerful poison](http://www.getty.edu/art/collection/objects/1206/attributed-to-annibale-fontana-drug-jar-for-theriac-italian-about-1580/?artview=dor5067), while elsewhere, even queens are not free from the [iron bars of prison](http://www.getty.edu/art/collection/objects/2600/jean-bourdichon-saint-avia-in-prison-receiving-communion-from-the-virgin-french-about-1480-1485/?dz=0.5000,0.5778,0.78) and the [judgment of the gods](http://www.getty.edu/art/collection/objects/105157/unknown-maker-athaliah-queen-of-judah-dragged-from-the-temple-french-about-1413-1415/?dz=0.2775,0.3170,1.82).
