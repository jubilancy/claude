> **noah's ark / the burning bush / allegorical image of the virgin mary as the enclosed garden and paradise of all delights**
>
> full-page miniatures from a manuscript originally created in east anglia, c. 1190-1200, containing a picture cycle of the life of christ. the book was transformed in northern england, c. 1480-90, when a second cycle of images and texts (rosary prayers and others) was integrated into the earlier cycle. the miniatures above make part of these late medieval additions. 
>
> source: Los Angeles, [J. Paul Getty Museum, Ms. 101](https://www.getty.edu/art/collection/object/109CRQ), ff. 10r, 13r, 79r
