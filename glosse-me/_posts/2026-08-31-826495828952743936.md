## Mechanical Art for Edward Gorey's First Doubleday Anchor Cover (1953)

Mechanical art for Edward Gorey's first Doubleday Anchor cover, created in 1953. Jason Epstein, Doubleday Anchor's co-founder, and his then-wife Barbara commissioned Gorey for the imprint's early cover designs.
